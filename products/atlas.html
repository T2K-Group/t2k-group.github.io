A complete unit

Tracker, connectivity and platform designed together.

Customers rent ATLAS trackers as complete operational units. They switch on and deploy the agreed equipment rather than selecting SIMs, changing firmware or assembling the underlying service themselves.

T2K Group remains responsible for shaping the hardware, connectivity and software configuration around the agreed use case.

IoT engineering in practice

One complete device-to-application path.

ATLAS shows how T2K Group applies end-to-end IoT product development to a public, operational product.

Tracking hardware→Embedded software→Multi-network connectivity→Cloud platform→Operational dashboard→Reporting & audit

Each layer affects the next: device behaviour shapes power and communications; connectivity shapes data age and recovery; the cloud and application must present status and uncertainty in a form operators can use.
