# T2K Group Website Overview

The T2K Group website positions the business primarily as a UK IoT engineering company specialising in connected products and operational systems. ATLAS is the principal public proof point for the wider hardware-to-cloud capability.

## `index.html` - Homepage

- SEO title: “IoT Engineering & Product Development UK | T2K Group”.
- H1: “IoT Engineering for Connected Products and Operational Systems”.
- Defines T2K as an end-to-end IoT product development company within the opening content.
- Covers connected hardware, embedded systems, firmware, device communications, cloud platforms, APIs, applications, integrations and deployment.
- Includes a “Selected Engineering Work” section using ATLAS as a permission-safe public example.
- Shows the complete ATLAS path from tracking hardware to reporting and audit.
- Links to the IoT Engineering service page, ATLAS and substantive engineering articles.

## `engineering/index.html` - IoT Engineering

- Main commercial landing page for custom IoT engineering and product development.
- SEO title: “Custom IoT Engineering & Product Development | T2K Group”.
- H1: “Custom IoT Engineering”.
- Covers requirements and architecture, IoT hardware, embedded systems, firmware, connectivity, device communications, cloud infrastructure, telemetry, APIs, applications, integrations, prototyping, testing and deployment.
- Provides a six-stage engagement path from problem framing to validation in context.
- Links individual engineering stages to relevant Blog articles.

## `products/atlas.html` - ATLAS

- Provides a concise group-level introduction to ATLAS and links to the dedicated ATLAS website.
- Explains the complete rented tracker service and operational platform.
- Includes an “IoT engineering in practice” architecture showing hardware, embedded software, connectivity, cloud, dashboard and reporting.
- Avoids duplicating the deeper industry and product content owned by the ATLAS website.

## `about/index.html` - About

- Describes T2K Group as a UK IoT product and engineering organisation.
- Explains the hardware-to-cloud capability and relationship between T2K Group and ATLAS.
- Adds Organisation structured data with verified subject areas and UK market context.
- Includes named engineering profiles for Jack Fitton and Dominik Powell.
- Covers Jack’s University of York engineering Master’s degree, public-sector and product-design experience, Labman Multivial electronics work, authorship of ATLAS and leadership of T2K electronics programmes.
- Covers Dominik’s University of York education, robotics engineering work at DLR in Germany, and understanding of firmware, software and operational requirements.
- Adds Person structured data for both engineers without inventing photographs, customer projects or legal details.

## `contact/index.html` - Contact

- Routes ATLAS, IoT engineering, partnership and general enquiries.
- Prompts visitors for operational context, environment and outcomes.
- Uses an honest draft-form state until an approved delivery endpoint is connected.
- Still requires approved email, telephone and registered-company details before publication.

## `blog/` - IoT Engineering Insights

- Repositioned around IoT requirements, architecture, connectivity and prototyping.
- Current substantive articles:
  - `choosing-iot-connectivity.html` - choosing cellular, Wi-Fi, LoRaWAN or short-range connectivity from operational requirements.
  - `start-with-the-operation.html` - IoT requirements and system framing.
  - `hardware-software-boundaries.html` - IoT architecture and system integration.
  - `prototype-the-uncertainty.html` - focused IoT prototyping and evidence.
- Each article has unique metadata, Article structured data, breadcrumb structured data, named-person authorship and an IoT Engineering enquiry route.
- Every article is visibly attributed to Jack Fitton and Dominik Powell and links to both About-page profiles.
- Article structured data identifies both authors as people with their T2K affiliations and University of York education; Jack’s supplied role is also included.

## `legal/`

- Contains draft Privacy and Website Terms pages.
- Both require legal entity, registered-company and contact details before publication.

## Shared design and production SEO

- `assets/styles.css` contains the shared responsive layout, proof-flow component, navigation, cards, forms and article styles.
- `assets/site.js` controls mobile navigation and the draft enquiry-form response.
- `assets/T2K_Logo.svg` is the only non-icon image used by the site.
- Public pages are indexable and use canonical URLs on `https://t2k.group`.
- `robots.txt` allows crawlers and advertises the XML sitemap.
- `sitemap.xml` contains every public canonical URL.
