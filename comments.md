Core SEO Positioning

T2K should be positioned primarily as a:

UK IoT engineering company specialising in connected products and operational systems.

The hierarchy should be:

Primary category: IoT Engineering
Core service: End-to-end IoT product development
Capabilities: Hardware, embedded systems, connectivity, cloud, software, data and deployment
Differentiator: Systems designed for real operational environments
Proof point: ATLAS and other engineering projects

A strong summary statement would be:

T2K designs and develops complete IoT systems, from connected hardware and embedded software through connectivity, cloud platforms and operational applications.

Best SEO Opportunity

I would not try to make T2K rank for very broad terms like:

engineering company

Instead, aim to establish a strong niche around:

End-to-end IoT engineering for connected products and operational systems.

That gives you a clear commercial topic while allowing T2K to demonstrate depth across:

Hardware → Firmware → Connectivity → Cloud → Software → Deployment

The three biggest improvements I'd make first are:

Make “IoT Engineering” the primary category across the homepage and engineering page.
Add real project evidence and use ATLAS as the flagship proof of T2K's end-to-end capability.
Build a genuinely technical IoT knowledge base written by named T2K engineers.

That would give T2K a much clearer SEO identity and, more importantly, a much clearer proposition to potential engineering customers.
