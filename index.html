Your ATLAS deployment

A clear route to polling day.

Start with your election plan. Build a deployment around your stations, ballot boxes and count venue.

  1. Plan together

    Discuss your ballot box numbers, polling locations, count venue and election timetable with our team.

  2. Prepare your setup

    Agree tracker deployment, define operational sites and arrange access for the people who need it.

  3. Deploy and monitor

    Deploy the trackers with your ballot boxes and follow reported locations and return journeys in ATLAS.

  4. Review the election

    Use retained journey records and exports to review movements and inform your next deployment.
