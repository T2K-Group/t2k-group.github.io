Selected engineering work

ATLAS - IoT engineering in practice.

ATLAS is T2K Group’s principal product and a public example of our end-to-end IoT capability. It demonstrates a complete information path rather than an isolated prototype.

Tracking hardware→Embedded software→Multi-network connectivity→Cloud platform→Operational dashboard→Reporting & audit
Challenge

Make critical physical-asset movements visible and reviewable.

The system needs to connect field equipment with current operational information and retained journey history.

Engineering

Design the connected product as one service.

T2K is responsible for the tracker, firmware, connectivity behaviour, data path, platform, interfaces and reporting model.

Operational constraints

Work beyond a controlled test environment.

Movement, intermittent mobile service, physical deployment, access control and clear data-age communication influence the complete design.

Outcome

A complete operational tracking platform.

ATLAS combines live asset visibility, retained movement history, access controls and reporting in one connected product service.
