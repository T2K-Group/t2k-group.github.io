Privacy Notice

Working privacy information for the T2K Group website. This page requires legal and company-detail review before publication.

Legal review required. Confirm the responsible legal entity, registered details, privacy contact, processing purposes, service providers and retention periods before launch.

1. Who is responsible

The final notice must identify the exact T2K Group legal entity responsible for personal information collected through this website and explain how to contact it.

2. Information we may collect

The website may collect information submitted through an enquiry form, including names, work contact details, organisation information and the content of the enquiry. Basic technical and security information may also be processed when the website is accessed.

3. Why information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess potential ATLAS or engineering requirements, maintain website security and meet applicable legal obligations. The lawful basis for each activity must be confirmed in the approved notice.

4. Sharing, retention and rights

The final notice must explain relevant service providers, any group-company access, international transfers, retention periods and the rights available under applicable data-protection law.

5. Contact and complaints

Add the approved privacy contact and the correct information about raising a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
