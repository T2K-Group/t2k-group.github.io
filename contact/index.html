What happens next

A focused first conversation.

The purpose of the first discussion is to understand fit and identify a sensible next step-not to force a technical specification before the need is clear.

01

Review the requirement

We look at the operational need, context and whether it aligns with ATLAS or T2K Group’s engineering capability.

02

Clarify the important unknowns

We identify the questions, constraints and dependencies that affect the technical route.

03

Agree an appropriate next step

That may be an ATLAS discussion, a discovery activity, a feasibility review or no further action if the fit is not right.
