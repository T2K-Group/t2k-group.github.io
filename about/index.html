About T2K Group

A UK IoT Engineering Company Built Around Real Operations

T2K Group develops complete connected systems across hardware, embedded software, connectivity, cloud platforms and operational applications. ATLAS is our principal product and a working example of that capability.

Why T2K Group

We look at the whole operational system.

Connected technology rarely succeeds because of one component. The physical asset, device, power, signal, data flow, software interface and operating process all influence the outcome.

Our role is to make those dependencies visible, choose an appropriate route and keep the work connected to the people and environment it is intended to support.

03Evidence over assumptionUse prototypes, review points and realistic testing to address uncertainty.

Jack graduated from the University of York with a Master’s degree in Engineering and has worked across public-sector and product-design roles since 2016. Working principles

Clear thinking before unnecessary complexity.

These principles guide both our own product development and the way we approach customer engineering.

Make the problem explicit

Define the users, environment, constraints and decision the technology needs to improve.

Design the interfaces

Treat the handovers between hardware, data, software and people as first-class engineering concerns.

Build for review

Work in stages that expose risk, invite useful feedback and allow evidence to shape the next decision.
